My story

From a curious kid to a full-stack builder.

I first became interested in IT in the fifth grade. For the next few years I kept learning — how computers work, how websites are made, and how a few lines of code can become something people actually use.

By the ninth grade I had started building my own projects. I learned frontend development, then Python, then the joy of connecting a real interface to a real database. Today I work across the stack: HTML, CSS, SCSS, SASS, JavaScript, Python, Django and Node.js.

I have studied online through Mohirdev, UpoUnity and other trusted platforms, and I keep learning by reading technical articles, experimenting with new tools and rebuilding things until they feel right.
